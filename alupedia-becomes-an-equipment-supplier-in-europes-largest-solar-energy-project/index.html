Alupedia Becomes an Equipment Supplier in Europe’s Largest Solar Energy Project

Leipzig, Germany – A new milestone in the energy transition, Energiepark Witznitz stands out as Europe’s largest solar park with an installed capacity of 650 MW and more than 1.1 million solar panels. Developed by MOVE ON Energy, this massive project serves as a model not only for Germany but also for Europe’s energy future.

Alupedia contributed to the very heart of this transition by supplying equipment for this landmark project. With advanced construction solutions and connection equipment, Alupedia supported the safe, durable, and long-lasting installation of the solar power plant.

Located on former mining lands in the south of Leipzig, this project by MOVE ON Energy has become one of the most concrete examples of the shift from coal to clean energy. Beyond providing clean power to the region, the investment has also contributed to employment and sustainable development.

With this collaboration, Alupedia has further strengthened its strong position in the European energy market, once again proving the value of its innovative engineering solutions on a global scale.

Statement from Alupedia:

“We are proud to take part in one of the most important projects of the energy transition. With the equipment we provide, contributing to sustainable energy infrastructure is a strategic mission for us. We will continue to invest in the energy of the future.”
